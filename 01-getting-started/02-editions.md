# Editions

## Select a desktop environment

The desktop environment is what the desktop and graphical environment looks like. Every edition gets the same PikaOS base. Choose a familiar desktop experience or jump straight into a modern tiling workflow.

### GNOME

Focused, calm and a good first stop if you want the computer to stay out of your way.

#### KDE Plasma

Familiar, deeply adjustable and happy to become exactly your desktop.

#### Hyprland

A polished tiling setup with the motion and flexibility already dialled in.


#### Niri

Scrollable tiling for keyboard-first work, preconfigured so you can learn by using it.


#### COSMIC

A modern Rust-built desktop with tiling, workspaces and room to grow.

Use the NVIDIA ISO for RTX cards and GTX 1650 or newer.
