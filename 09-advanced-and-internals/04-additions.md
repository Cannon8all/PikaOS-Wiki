# Additions

- `prime-run` to enable optimus in cli applications.
- Added a GPU selector for KDE Plasma in systems with multiple GPUs.
- scx and ananicy-cpp included by default.
- PikaOS Kernel Manager with ability to change scx scheds.
- pika-hhd-meta with gamescope-session and inputplumber.
- OBS Gamecapture and multiatium available in repos.
- Many device specific additions such as (lact, asusctl, supergfx, corectrl, nvidia-dlss-switcher, xone, xpadneo, etc..)
- vulkan switcher wrappers for AMDGPUs
- optional amdgpu backlight blacklister package (breaks brightness controls on 99% of AMD Laptops, fixes them on some lenovo AMD laptops though)
- pika-multimedia-meta: meta package to get all codecs, gstreamer, vaapi, and vdpau drivers, in both 64 and 32 bit form to ensure proper hardware accelerated playback.
- pika-gaming-meta: fetches the multi media meta package in addition to wine-staging, steam, lutris, and many more....
- and many many more...
